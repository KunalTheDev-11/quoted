You type

Situation Matcher

Type or speak what’s going on, in your own words. Quoted picks an original quote that fits and adds a one-line reason. Save it to your vault or share the card.

Daily Quote Duel

Two original quotes, one vote a day. Pick the stronger one, then see how other readers voted and share your result.

Mental Vault

Keep the quotes that land. Search them, sort by newest, oldest, A–Z or Golden Wisdom, listen to any quote read aloud, and back up the whole vault as a text file.

Social Studio

Turn a quote into a Story (9:16), square post (1:1) or banner (16:9). Choose a gradient or wallpaper, set the type, add a contrast mask, and share in HD.

Made for daily reading

Full-screen quote cards in Dark, Light or Sepia. Auto-advance every 5, 10 or 15 seconds, get a daily quote notification, switch between English and Hinglish, and keep reading offline.

Every quote is original.
