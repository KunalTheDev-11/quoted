Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. Applies to the Quoted Android app (com.kunalthedev.quoted).

You don’t need an account to use Quoted. Your saved quotes, history and settings stay on your device. The text you type into the Situation Matcher is sent to Google’s Gemini service to find your match. The free app shows ads from Google.

Who we are

Quoted is developed by kunalthedev (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy can go to YOUR_EMAIL@example.com.

What stays on your device

These are stored only in the app’s local storage and are not sent to us:

  • Your Mental Vault (saved quotes, with the date you saved them).
  • The history of quotes you have already seen, used so you don’t get repeats.
  • Your settings: theme, animation style, haptics, slideshow timer, language and notification schedule.
  • Your Daily Quote Duel vote.
  • Quotes and wallpapers cached for offline reading.

What is sent to other companies

Situation Matcher (Google Gemini). When you describe a situation, the text you enter is sent over HTTPS to Google’s Gemini API, along with a small set of candidate quotes, so it can choose or write a match and explain it. Don’t enter anything you wouldn’t want processed this way, such as names, contact details or health information. Google handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. If you are offline or the request fails, the app matches on your device using keywords instead, and nothing is sent.

Voice input. If you speak your situation instead of typing it, your device’s speech recognition service turns your speech into text. That service is provided by your device maker or Google, and its handling of audio is governed by their policies, not ours.

Ads (Google). Quoted shows Google native ads. Google and its partners may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address and basic device and app information to show and measure ads, and may personalize them. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Read aloud. Text-to-speech uses the speech engine installed on your device.

Permissions

  • Internet: to reach the Situation Matcher and load ads.
  • Notifications: to deliver your daily quote, only if you turn it on.
  • Microphone: only if you choose voice input, and only when you tap it.

Sharing and exports

When you share a quote, a Social Studio image or a vault backup, the content goes to the app you choose, such as Instagram, WhatsApp or your files. After that, that app’s privacy policy applies. Quoted doesn’t upload your exports anywhere.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask you to create an account or give us your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t read your contacts, messages or photos.

Keeping and deleting your data

Delete a saved quote from the Mental Vault at any time. Under Settings, “Reset Recommendation Memory” clears your seen-quote history and “Reset All Settings” restores the defaults. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. We don’t hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove data held by Google, use your Google account’s privacy controls.

Children

Quoted is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will act on it.

Security

Requests to the Situation Matcher use encrypted connections. Data on your device is protected by your device’s own security, such as your screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.

Contact

Email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with any question or request about your privacy.
