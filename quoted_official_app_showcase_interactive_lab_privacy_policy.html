📊 SUMMARY OF DATA PRACTICES (GOOGLE PLAY DATA SAFETY OVERVIEW)
Account / Registration Required? No. Zero sign-up or personal profile required.
Mental Vault & Seen Quotes Storage: 100% On-Device Local SQLite / Android Room Database (`AppDatabase v3`).
AI Situation Matcher Prompts: Processed ephemerally via HTTPS by Google Gemini API solely to return your quote match.
User Data Deletion Controls: Built-in 1-tap deletion in `Settings → Data Vault` or by uninstalling the app.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Stored

1.1 Local On-Device Data (Android Room Database & SharedPreferences)

All core personalization data generated during your use of Quoted is stored locally on your Android device inside the application's private sandboxed storage using Android Room Persistence Library (AppDatabase v3) and SharedPreferences:

  • Mental Vault / Saved Quotes (favorite_quotes table): Quotes you save as favorites, along with their category, language code, and save timestamp (savedAt).
  • Reading History (seen_quotes table): Texts and timestamps of quotes you have already explored so the app avoids showing duplicates.
  • Offline Horizon Cache (cached_quotes table): Pre-fetched quotes cached locally for seamless offline reading.
  • App Preferences & Context Settings (AiContextPreferences & QuoteDuelPreferences): Your chosen visual theme (Dark, Light, Sepia), language toggle (English / Hinglish), haptic feedback preference, slideshow timer settings, mood/weather context selections, and Daily Quote Duel votes.

We do not operate a proprietary cloud database that harvests or backs up your private Mental Vault or reading history. This data remains strictly on your device unless you manually choose to export or share it using the app's Backup Vault or Social Studio features.

1.2 AI Situation Matcher Inputs (Google Gemini API)

When you voluntarily use the AI Situation Matcher feature and type or dictate a description of your current life situation (for example, "I have an interview in an hour"), that text prompt and candidate quotes are transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) to the Google Gemini Generative AI API (https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/) solely to select or craft a matching original quote and generate a 1-line explanation of why it fits your situation.

  • Your situational text prompt is not linked to your name, email, or personal identity.
  • We do not store, log, or sell your situational prompts on any developer-owned server.
  • Please avoid entering sensitive personal identifiers (such as passwords, financial account numbers, or medical record numbers) into the situation prompt box.

1.3 Voice Input & Text-to-Speech (TTS) Audio

Quoted allows you to listen to quotes read aloud using Android's built-in system android.speech.tts.TextToSpeech engine and optionally dictate situational prompts using your keyboard or system speech recognizer. All Text-to-Speech synthesis happens locally on your device. Quoted never records, stores, or uploads raw audio recordings of your voice to any developer server.

1.4 Advertising & Crash/Performance Diagnostics (Google AdMob)

To support the continued development of original content while keeping Quoted free to use, the app integrates Google AdMob (NativeAdPreloader) to display periodic native advertisements within the quote stream. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device Advertising ID (Google Play Ad ID), which you can reset or opt out of at any time in your Android device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Coarse IP-based location, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics (impressions and clicks) for fraud prevention and ad frequency capping.

2. Android Permissions Requested by Quoted

Quoted follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary for its features to function:

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to communicate with the Google Gemini API for live AI Situation Matching, fetch updated quote packages, and load Google AdMob Native Ads. (The app remains 100% functional offline using its 300+ pre-seeded quotes and Room cache.)
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional) Requested on Android 13+ solely if you enable scheduled Daily Quote Notifications in Settings → Haptics & Reading. You can revoke this at any time.
android.permission.VIBRATE Used to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback when flipping quotes, voting in Daily Quote Duels, or saving to the Mental Vault. Controllable via the in-app Haptics toggle.
Android System Share Sheet (ACTION_SEND) When you export graphics from the Social Studio or backup your Mental Vault, Quoted uses Android's secure system share intent (FileProvider / ACTION_SEND) without requiring broad read access to your personal photo library.

3. Data Retention, User Control & Deletion ("Data Vault")

You have complete ownership and instantaneous control over all data stored by Quoted on your device:

  • Delete Individual or All Saved Quotes: Open the Mental Vault inside the app to remove individual quotes or clear your entire saved collection (deleteAllFavorites()).
  • Reset Recommendation Memory: Navigate to Settings → Data Vault → Reset Recommendation Memory to permanently erase your seen quotes history (clearAllSeen()).
  • Reset All Settings to Default: Navigate to Settings → Data Vault → Reset All Settings to Default to wipe all local preferences, mood/context configurations, and duel state.
  • Complete Uninstall / Clear Storage: Uninstalling Quoted or clearing the app's storage in Android System Settings immediately and permanently deletes the entire local SQLite Room database and all associated preferences from your device.

4. Third-Party Services & SDK Links

Quoted integrates the following trusted third-party services governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API / Google Generative AI: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Security Measures

All network requests between Quoted and Google APIs are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Local data is isolated within Android's application sandbox, inaccessible to other apps on non-rooted devices.

Quoted is a general-audience wisdom and inspiration app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age of consent). If you believe any personal information was inadvertently provided, please contact us immediately.
